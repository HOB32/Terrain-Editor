Snow
The ground and grass snow is in the textures, so it is always there. The snow shader is the game's own, driven by the temperature: none above 35 °F, full at 30 °F; the server lowers it from "From" to "To" over "Gathers", so roofs, cars and fences whiten as the match goes on (0 = snowy from the start). Falling snow and fireworks are spawned around each player by the game itself.
